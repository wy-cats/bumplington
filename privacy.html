Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy covers the Bumplington Uploader, a private tool used only by the owner of the Bumplington YouTube channel.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to manage their titles, descriptions, privacy settings and playlists. It does not access any other person's account or data.

What is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, so uploads can continue without signing in each time. The tool does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Children

The Bumplington videos are made for children, but this tool is used only by the adult channel owner and never collects information from viewers.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Bumplington channel page.
